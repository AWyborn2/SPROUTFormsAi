OVERBURDEN REMOVAL & FOREST DISEASE

Loading is easiest on a downward slope; work systematically to avoid re-travelling scraped ground. Two forest diseases live in the soil you move — open both.

Dieback +

Lives in top and gravel soils, roots and plant matter; spread mostly by moist soil movement. Spores are unlikely to survive above 25°. Brushing down at designated wash-down locations is permitted when dry conditions prevail.

Armillaria +

Lives in topsoil and plant matter; spread by dry, dusty soil movement and strong winds over disturbed ground. Wash down is required at all times.

Known disease areas are identified in State Forest; all private land is disease-suspect. Not washing or brushing down correctly can spread disease. Design area: every area has a GPS design file that must be followed. Confused about a design? Stop and ask the area Supervisor.
