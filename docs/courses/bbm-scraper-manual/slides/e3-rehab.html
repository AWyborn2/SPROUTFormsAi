REHABILITATION

Rehabilitating a mined area to original condition is an obligation once mining is complete.

10 m

300 mm
Max apron opening when ejecting on slopes
  • Always travel straight up or straight down steep slopes.
  • The rehab area is scarified on contour to reduce wash-outs and seeded at the same time.
  • Where a batter is too steep for scraper work, material is stockpiled at the base and a track dozer spreads it up the slope.
