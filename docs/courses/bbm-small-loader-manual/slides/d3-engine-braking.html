ENGINE BRAKING

When travelling down hills, shift down to 1st gear and run the engine at low speed, using the engine as the brake. The service brake pedal assists with speed control — it is not the only thing slowing you.

If the engine revs begin to climb, applying the brake pedal (centre foot pedal) helps control engine speed. Failing to brake over-revs the engine and damages it.

Never the impeller pedal

Do not use the impeller pedal for downhill braking — its initial stage increases ground speed.

Service brake alone? No.

Controlling speed on the service brake only cooks the brakes and leaves nothing in reserve. Gear down, let the engine hold you, brake to assist.
