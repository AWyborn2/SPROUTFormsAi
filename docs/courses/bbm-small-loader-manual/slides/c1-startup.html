START UP — OPERATOR SEQUENCE

Follow the OEM start-up procedure for the machine. These BBM rules sit on top of it.

1

Adjust the seat, apply the seat belt, and confirm the park brake is engaged and the transmission is in neutral.

2

Turn the key to ON and watch the monitoring system perform its self-test.

3

Sound the horn once, then wait 5–10 seconds before starting the engine — anyone near the machine has time to clear.

4

Listen for unusual noises and check that all warning-panel lights are out.

5

Do not let the machine idle for more than 20 minutes — it glazes the cylinder liners and shortens engine life.

6

Before you leave the park-up, complete the operational safety checks — steering, brakes, kick-outs (next slides).
