ENGINE BRAKING

When travelling down hills, shift down to 1st gear and run the engine at low speed, using the brake pedal to assist with speed control.

Travel down the slope with the engine holding you back. If the engine revs begin to climb, applying the brake pedal (centre foot pedal) helps control engine speed. Failing to brake over-revs the engine and damages it.

Never the impeller pedal

Do not use the impeller pedal for downhill braking — its initial stage increases ground speed.

The service brake controls your speed

The service brake pedal is what controls the loader's speed when travelling. Downhill, 1st gear and low revs keep the engine from over-revving while you brake.
