ACCESS, EGRESS & LEAVING THE CAB

Three points of contact

The safest way on and off the loader is three points of contact — two hands and a foot, or two feet and a hand — on the steps and handrails, facing the machine.

Do not use the steering wheel or control levers as a hand-hold for three-point contact.

Check steps and handrails for condition on every pre-start.

Leaving the operator’s seat

  • Park on flat, level, fundamentally stable ground.
  • Ground the bucket and shut the engine down.
  • Apply all safety locks and the park brake — this stops the control levers being knocked as you leave the cab.
  • Make sure the engine is shut down completely before descending to ground level.
