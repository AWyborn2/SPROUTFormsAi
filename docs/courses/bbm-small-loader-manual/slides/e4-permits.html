EXCAVATION / PENETRATION PERMITS

Certain areas of the mine need an Excavation Permit as part of clearance to work. Before digging in or around them, obtain a Ground Excavation Permit from an Authorised Excavation Officer. It is kept at the job site at all times and must be accompanied by a JHA.

Permit areas include

  • Workshops and surrounds
  • Fixed Plant facilities and surrounds
  • Field Offices and surrounds
  • Both sides of the bitumen access road (Quindanning Rd to the old Saddleback Production office)
  • Tunnel Road beside the Saddleback bore field
  • The Marradong OBC access road and facilities

The GPS system (when fitted) references all facilities and services in the planned work area. Unsure? Contact your Supervisor before starting.

If you dig up an underground service

  1. Stop work and move back from the area.
  2. Inform your Supervisor immediately.
  3. Do not continue until the area has been re-inspected.
