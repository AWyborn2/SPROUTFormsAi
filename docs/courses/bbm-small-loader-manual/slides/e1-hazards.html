UNEVEN GROUND & OBSTACLES

Operating on uneven ground, or where there are obstacles, these points must be adhered to:

  • Ensure your travel path is clear of obstacles.
  • Operate at a safe speed for the work area and avoid sudden changes in direction.
  • Avoid travelling over large rocks, fallen trees, tree stumps and similar obstacles.
  • Either use the bucket to remove them or travel around them.

Built-up and confined areas

Ask for a spotter whenever visibility is obstructed or the area is confined.

Power lines

Clearances are set out in the Service Road Drivers Permit Manual, “Overhead Powerlines”. If unsure while working near power lines, check with your Supervisor first.
