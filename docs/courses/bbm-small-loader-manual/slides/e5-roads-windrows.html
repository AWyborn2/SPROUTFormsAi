ROAD MAINTENANCE · WINDROW CONSTRUCTION

Spillage removal, windrow and sump construction, bush-track maintenance. Working on any road, inform all road users before you start and consider signage for the working area (Service Road Drivers Permit Manual).

Build it straight

A windrow is a physical barrier and marks the road edge.

Build and maintain it in a straight line. Don’t stagger windrows at sump entrances or intersections. At a sump entrance the windrow runs straight to the entrance, and the far side gets a tapered end to deflect normal traffic.

+ The rules

Straight-face it

Stood up close to vertical so it deflects a vehicle rather than ramping it.

Travel along the windrow and “side cut” it with the bucket, stopping to empty the bucket as it fills to avoid tyre damage. A sloped face acts as a ramp if struck.

+ The technique

Cap it last

Only once the basic structure is built or repaired.

Set the bucket at the desired height, crowded flat or slightly aggressive. Drive in slowly so the bucket “dozes” off the top. Don’t dump — reverse out at the same height and repeat along the windrow; the material in the bucket dozes the next section or fills small variations. Use the boom and bucket kick-outs to hold the height.
