HOPPER CLEAN-UP — THE SEQUENCE

The ROM / hopper is a restricted area with haul trucks tipping into it. Nothing enters until every step below is complete.

1

Complete a risk assessment (RAP) for the clean-up.

2

Gain permission from the Duty Production Supervisor before entering the ROM / hopper restricted area.

3

Call the Control Room (crusher control), inform them of the clean-up and ask for the orange lights to be activated.

4

Notify pit control and inform the haul truck operators — a general broadcast — directing them to the ROM.

5

Only when all of the above is complete may the loader enter the hopper area and perform the clean-up — 40 m exclusion and positive radio communication throughout.

6

After the clean-up: ask the Control Room to restore the green lights at the crusher, then inform the Duty Production Supervisor and pit control, who tell the truck operators once green is back.
