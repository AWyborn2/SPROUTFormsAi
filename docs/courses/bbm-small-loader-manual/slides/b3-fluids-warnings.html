FLUIDS & WARNINGS

Checked by sight glass

The hydraulic oil, transmission fluid and coolant levels on the loader are read from their sight glasses — no need to open anything hot or pressurised.

Engine oil is the exception: check it on the dipstick.

Low levels, or a level that keeps dropping between checks, go on the defect report.

Engine oil warning light stays on after start

At start-up every warning-panel light should go out. If the oil light stays illuminated, all three actions apply:

  1. Shut the engine off.
  2. Report the fault to pit control.
  3. Tag the loader “out of service”.
