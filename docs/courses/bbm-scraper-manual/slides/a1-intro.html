WHAT A SCRAPER DOES AT BBM

Scrapers develop the areas to be mined — stripping topsoil and gravel down to the top of hard cap, stockpiling that material for later use, or replacing it to rehabilitate mined ground.

They usually work independently, but can push-pull as a pair or be pushed by a track dozer. Thorough planning keeps topsoil and gravel stockpiled separately, with room for the volume being moved.

Safety is the critical function. BBM ground means cross-slope and down-slope travel — travel restrictions always apply.
