MACHINE CONDITION — WHILE OPERATING

Condition is judged from many signs. Open each one — and notify Pit Control or your Supervisor if you recognise any abnormality.

New damage, loose fixtures, leaks or cracks

Anything visual that was not there at pre-start. Stop and report — don’t work on with a growing fault.

+ What to look for

A change in a temperature or pressure gauge

Gauges drifting from where they normally sit — heat, oil or hydraulic pressure — are early warnings.

+ What to look for

Changes in the sound of the engine or turbo

A new whine, knock or surge. Listen at start-up too — any unusual noise is a reason to stop.

+ What to look for

Cab vibrations

Vibration you don’t normally feel points to driveline, tyre or mounting problems.

+ What to look for

Changes in exhaust gas colour

Black, blue or white smoke that isn’t normal for the machine means combustion, oil or coolant trouble.

+ What to look for

Differences in the response of the control levers

Slow, jerky or dead hydraulic response. Don’t keep working a loader whose controls have changed.
