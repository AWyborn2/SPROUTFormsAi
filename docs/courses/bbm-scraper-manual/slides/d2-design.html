Topsoil pile height limit (environmental)

Windrow edge heights

Working on-side: min 300 mm. Working off-side: min 500 mm. When not being worked: a 500 mm edge windrow.

Keep the "V"

Edges slope inwards and higher than the middle, "V"-shaped at all times — with the scraper leaning in, the risk of slipping off the edge drops sharply. If the drop zone levels out, reinstate the inward slope immediately.
