MISFIRES

A misfire is an explosive charge that has not exploded as designed. Be aware of the possibility in any blasted work area. If emulsion or a cast booster is unearthed, acknowledge each step:

Stop work and notify your Supervisor immediately.
Move away from the face, barricade the area, and keep a minimum 10 metre clearance.
The Supervisor informs the Quarry Manager and Drill & Blast Supervisor, who take control of the area and the removal of the explosives.
Never handle, move or bury a suspected misfire yourself. Drill-track work follows the Exclusion Zone and Drill & Blast Track SWIs.
