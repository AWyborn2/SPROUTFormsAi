BEFORE YOU START
1

Work through each module

Six modules, unlocked in order from the Module Menu. Finish one and you return to the menu for the next.

2

Answer the graded questions

Each module ends with its theory questions — the same sixteen that are on your assessment paper. Your answers are recorded as you go.

3

Watch for the green tick

A slide ticks green once it is read, its cards opened, or its question answered — then Next lights up. Finish all six modules to hand your theory in.

This manual is the minimum standard for small-loader work at BBM. Read it in full — every theory question is drawn from what is in here and the Mine Site SME Operating Manual.
