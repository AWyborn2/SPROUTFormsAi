SCRAPER STOCKPILES

Stockpiles at BBM move overburden — the topsoil and gravel removed from pits before mining, and reused later to rehabilitate mined areas.

Topsoil — the first 100-150 mm of material, stockpiled separately from gravel.
Gravel — removed down to cap-rock height, or to the design level where "gravel ore" begins.
Vermeer cuttings — waste rock and gravel cut to a smooth profile.
