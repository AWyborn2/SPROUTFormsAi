QUICK HITCH · REHABILITATION

Quick hitch locking pins

Whenever a bucket or forks are fitted, check the quick hitch locking pins are fully inserted before you lift or travel.

Pins that are not fully home can let the attachment come off — with the bucket raised, that is a dropped load next to people and machines.

Moving forest salvage on forks

Small loaders move forest litter during clearing and rehabilitation. Because it is so different from normal loader work, these rules apply at all times:

  • Moving or transporting salvage is restricted to daylight hours only.
  • Timber must not protrude more than 2 metres beyond each side of the forks.
  • In confined areas ensure clearance on both sides so the load touches nothing.
  • Take care when crowding the forks back — logs and branches must not come back toward the cab.
  • Travel with the load as low to the ground as possible.
  • Always travel up or down slopes — never across a slope with a load.
