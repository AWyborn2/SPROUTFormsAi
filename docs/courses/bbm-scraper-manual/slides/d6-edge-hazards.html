THE STOCKPILE EDGE — WHERE IT GOES WRONG

Edge too close to a face. Gravel rill and windrow have collapsed into a sump. Keep a vertical edge below the base >5 m away, or reclaim with an excavator.

Edge too high to retrieve. A large rock in the windrow plus spinning wheels slides the scraper off. Scrapers have slipped off stockpiles — stay off soft edges.

Design the drop. Manage edge drop-heights to design — keep approaches straight and edges maintained as the pile is worked.

Never lower a windrow greater than 500 mm with the scraper. If the edge (windrow) grows over 500 mm, a track dozer or grader reduces it first.
