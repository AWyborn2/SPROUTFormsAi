AUTHORISED USE OF SME

No person operates any piece of SME or other equipment at BBM unless they have been authorised to do so and are deemed competent to operate it in that particular task or situation.

Operators must hold a current Authority to Operate. This manual covers every loader at BBM under 80 tonne, across BBM operational areas and private land holdings.

Clearing & burning? You must also hold Q50073241 BBM Clearing and Burning – Practical before operating any SME for clearing or burning.

Operators and Supervisors are responsible for

  • The safe operation of SME, RGVs and LVs.
  • Following the procedures in this manual.
  • Reporting all hazards, near misses and incidents as soon as possible.

Pre-start inspections follow the Mine Site SME Operating Manual; road rules follow the Mine Site Drivers Permit Manual.
