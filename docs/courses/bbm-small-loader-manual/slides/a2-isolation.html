ISOLATION & TAGS

Acknowledge each rule — these are checked on your practical demonstration.

Isolate the loader with a hasp and your personnel lock attached before entering the footprint of the machine.
Conduct a try test to prove the isolation before you step inside the footprint.
An “Out of Service” tag on the isolation switch means exactly that — do not operate the loader, and report the tag to pit control.
Never ignore or remove another person’s tag so you can operate. A tag is only removed by the person or process that placed it.
