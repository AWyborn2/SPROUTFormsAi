MOVING OFF — OPERATOR SEQUENCE

Failing to watch your working environment while tramming or working is how a loader hits an obstacle — or another machine.

1

Check the area surrounding the loader. In an active area (not an SME park-up) make sure the 40-metre exclusion zone is clear — every piece of working equipment at BBM has one.

2

Seat belt firm and comfortable. Throttle switch to high idle, if fitted.

3

Sound the horn twice, then wait 5–10 seconds before moving any implement or moving forward.

4

Raise the bucket to about 300–400 mm off the ground for unobstructed travel.

5

With the impeller pedal (left foot) applied, release the park brake and select the gear you want to travel in.

6

Release the impeller pedal gently to move off, lifting your foot completely to let the machine reach the desired speed. Stop the machine before any transmission direction change.
