HAUL ROUTE PREPARATION
  • On a cross slope, keep the track level or sloping back towards the hill, and wide enough for safe one-way travel.
  • Where there is an edge or across-slope route, build and maintain a 500 mm windrow on the daylight edge.
  • Don't cut a cross-slope track with a scraper — use a track machine or grader. Bowl-grade with the apron slightly open and the ejector forward.
  • When a grader passes, re-establish the 40 m exclusion zone for each passing manoeuvre.
  • If dust restricts visibility or drifts to neighbours, stop — use a water cart and restore visibility before continuing.
20%
Maximum up/down slope for scrapers — beyond this needs an approved risk assessment.
