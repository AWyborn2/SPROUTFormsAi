EMERGENCY PROCEDURE

If a fire is detected on the loader you are operating, every one of these actions applies, in this order. Your assessor will ask you to demonstrate or explain it.

1

Stop the loader and ground the bucket.

2

Select neutral and engage the park brake.

3

Activate the fire suppression system.

4

Call the emergency.

5

Evacuate the area.

Emergency exit

If the door is blocked, the right-hand side cab window is your emergency exit point. Know how it releases before you ever need it.

Parking and shutdown

Park in the designated location, ground the G.E.T, park brake on, let the engine idle down, shut down completely before climbing down, close doors and windows, then the post-operational walk-around and defect report.
