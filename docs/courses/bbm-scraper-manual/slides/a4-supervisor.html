SUPERVISION, SIGNAGE & ACCESS

Open each card to see how the area is set up and controlled.

Supervisor inspection +

Before work starts: confirm operators are competent, provide safe roads and turnarounds, confirm the method of operation, minimise interaction with LVs/RGVs, and address hazards before work can begin or continue.

Access to scraper work areas +

Demarcate as close as possible to the actual area of operation — not the whole pit. Access by anyone other than the area WAPL or Contractor Supervisor is granted only once all scrapers have stopped, and work resumes only when the vehicle has left or is parked in an agreed, acknowledged location.

Managing signage +

Access-control signs may stay up during breaks but come down at end of shift once the area is safe. If a hazard can't be made safe, the area stays controlled. If the area is left open overnight, signs may stay — laid text face-down and off the road or track.

Interaction with other equipment +

Where other equipment works within the designated scraper area, the 40-metre exclusion zone applies — unless the work areas are separated by a physical barrier.
