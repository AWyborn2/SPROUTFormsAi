OPERATIONAL SAFETY CHECKS

Every shift, before you move far from the park-up and with the 40 m exclusion zone clear, prove these systems work as per OEM specification. Only proceed if all of them do.

Steering

Test left and right on both the primary and the secondary steering system. The secondary system is battery operated — it is your steering if the engine or main hydraulics fail.

Brake pedal

Test holding ability and accumulator pressure to the OEM specification for the machine — the park brake and the service brake, at the start of every shift.

Hydraulic functions

Test the bucket and boom kick-outs. Working kick-outs also help hold a set height when capping windrows.

Vision Windows and mirrors clean before operating. If dust or fog obscures your vision, stop until it clears. Know the loader’s blind spots.
No riders Do not allow other persons to ride on or in the small loader at any time.
