LOST OR DAMAGED HARDWARE

Ground Engaging Tools (G.E.T) that are lost into the dig can damage the crushers or the overland belt lines if they are mined and fed into the hoppers.

Buckets, blades and rippers are not designed to work unprotected — they will be damaged if used without their G.E.T.

Category A fault Lost or damaged G.E.T must be replaced before operations begin or recommence.

When you notice it

  1. Report immediately to Pit Control or the relevant Supervisor.
  2. Explore every safe means of recovering the lost G.E.T to minimise damage and downtime.
  3. If it is not recovered, the area is demarcated by GPS co-ordinates or survey pick-up.
