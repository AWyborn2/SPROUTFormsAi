MACHINE INTERLOCKS

Three locks, three effects. Tap each to reveal what it does — apply all of them, plus the park brake, whenever you leave the seat.

STEERING LOCK tap to reveal
The steering is locked and forward and reverse gears are locked out.
IMPLEMENT LOCK tap to reveal
The implement (bucket and boom) is hydraulically locked — the levers cannot move it.
TRANSMISSION LOCK tap to reveal
The transmission is locked in neutral and will not “drive”.
