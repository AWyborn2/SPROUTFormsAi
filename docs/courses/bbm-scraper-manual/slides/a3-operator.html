OPERATOR ACCOUNTABILITIES

Operating articulated equipment across down- and cross-slope ground takes careful planning. Acknowledge each duty.

Locate the work area and complete a basic risk assessment before starting the task.
Stop immediately if in difficulty and consult the Duty WAPL Supervisor.
Always comply with the 40-metre exclusion zone.
Only control the area and grant access when delegated and your name is on the "Contact Job Coordinator" signs — if in doubt, refer to the Supervisor.
Report all hazards, near misses and incidents immediately.
