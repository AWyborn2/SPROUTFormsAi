STRIPPING GRAVEL

There is a real rollover risk when stripping gravel — and it climbs sharply once the gravel windrow height exceeds 500 mm.

Over 500 mm? Don't touch it with the scraper. A track machine or grader lowers the windrow first. Never attempt to lower a gravel windrow greater than 500 mm with the scraper.

Actual result of stripping a gravel windrow higher than 500 mm.
