PUSH-PULL — OPERATOR SEQUENCE
1

Lead scraper enters the cut (cushion hitch down), skims and loads, locks 2nd gear ~1200-1300 RPM so the following scraper can catch up and couple. Both must be in line.

2

After contacting the push pad, the following scraper lowers the cushion hitch and bail arm to complete hook-up, locks 2nd gear and applies full throttle to push. Lead sets the cut at ~router depth (100 mm).

3

As the lead lifts its bowl out of the cut, both scrapers hold full throttle.

4

Following scraper lowers its bowl into the lead's tails with a small side steer to slide the bale into the pulling position; lead raises its bowl to travel height and pulls.

5

When loaded, the following operator raises bowl, bail arm and cushion hitch, up-shifts to lift the bail off the hook, then steers aside and reduces speed for safe separation.

6

Make sure the following scraper is uncoupled before up-shifting. Don't dig deeper than the routers or overload the bowl — both operators stay ready to stop or disengage.
