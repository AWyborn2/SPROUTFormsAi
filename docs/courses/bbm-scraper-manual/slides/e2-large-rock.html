LARGE ROCK IN THE BOWL

Large rocks can injure the operator or damage the scraper on ejection. Move to the designated dump area, on level ground, and follow the sequence.

1

Open the apron no more than 300 mm — rocks under 300 mm won't be a problem, and this stops a rock being ejected onto a stockpile.

2

Stationary, turn the front 90° to the bowl (jack-knife), then lower the bowl until the front cutting edge is just off the surface.

3

Open the apron, then slide the ejector forward to eject the rock. Don't try to crush it — that damages the ejector, rollers and apron.

4

Raise the apron so the rock can be pushed out but not roll far; keep sliding the ejector until the rock rolls clear.

5

Hold the 90° configuration, select reverse and back slowly away — never reverse over the rock (it cuts or bruises the tyre).

6

When clear, straighten up, move off avoiding the rock, and continue operations. Place large rocks in an orderly line in the designated area.
