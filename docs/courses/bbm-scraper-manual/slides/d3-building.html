BUILDING STOCKPILES
  • Best edge compaction comes from scrapers travelling close to the edge, dragging material along it and rilling it over the side.
  • Graders or dozers maintain the V-shape and push an edge out — check the re-established edge can still hold the scraper's weight.
  • Never dump rock or tree roots on the edge. A cutting edge or router bit catching them slides the rear wheels off. Dump in the middle, or remove to the side.
Over 5 m high? Step the edges by a minimum of 5 m to create catch berms — they keep the edge stable as the pile is built or reclaimed.
