MACHINE CONDITION

Pre-start inspections are conducted as required by the Mine Site SME Operating Manual. The walk-around has one purpose with three parts: inspect for damage, identify any faults, and confirm the loader is safe to operate.

Ground level

  • External components, steering components, grease lines to the steering knuckles
  • Tyres, rims and wheel studs
  • Under the machine for fluid leaks
  • Steps and handrails

Fluids & systems

  • Hydraulic and transmission oil — sight glass
  • Coolant — sight glass
  • Engine oil — dipstick
  • Fire suppression system and extinguisher condition

In the cab

  • Components and gauges
  • Windows and mirrors — clean and undamaged
  • Work lights, seat and seatbelt
  • Then complete the pre-start checklist and defect report
