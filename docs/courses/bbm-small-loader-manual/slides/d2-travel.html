TRAVEL ON HAUL ROADS · LOADING

On the haul roads

  • The Mine Site Drivers Permit Manual road rules apply — signage, separation distances, overtaking procedure.
  • Be alert, listen to the two-way and use correct radio protocols; watch for other traffic.
  • Bucket level, 300–400 mm from the ground. Do not curl it into a “carry” position unless you are carrying material.
  • Follow the 40 m procedure whenever you work around other equipment.

Loading the bucket

When loading the bucket with material, operate the loader in 1st gear only — full torque, controlled speed, and the machine stays planted as the bucket fills.

Use controlled, smooth braking; stop the machine during transmission direction changes; manoeuvre with general awareness of everything around you.
