OPERATIONAL HAZARDS

If a scraper gets into difficulty

Be alert to problems and prepared to stop. If you need assistance at any time:

  • Stop the operation.
  • Notify other scrapers in the area to stop.
  • Inform the area WAPL Supervisor immediately.

Look after the track

Scrapers have limited suspension — a rough ride can injure the operator and damage the machine.

  • Keep the scraper track smooth and level.
  • Maintain it by bowl grading.
  • Ensure the cushion hitch activates and works correctly when travelling.
