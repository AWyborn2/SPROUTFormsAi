RECLAIMING STOCKPILES

Every reclaim

  • Inspect the pile from the safety of the middle before starting.
  • Always approach the intended cut straight on, and reclaim in straight sections only.
  • Stay a minimum of 1 m from any stockpile edge.
  • If the scraper starts to slip off, stop immediately and call the duty supervisor.

Truck / dozer / loader-built piles

  • Edges may not be compacted — a Section 44 WAPL supervisor inspects, and a JHA is in place, before starting.
  • No natural rill? A track dozer re-establishes it first.
  • Never cut down the outside edge with a scraper — a dozer or grader maintains the edge and removes oversize.
