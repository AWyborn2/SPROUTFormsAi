DOZER-ASSISTED & SELF-LOADING

Dozer-assisted

  • Scraper passes on the dozer's right (cab-side); establish visual or audible contact before passing close.
  • Lock 2nd gear, 1200-1300 RPM, cushion hitch down, cut at 100 mm. Don't over-cut.
  • Maintain momentum and let the dozer do the pushing; it sprags so the blade slides gently onto the push pad.
  • When loaded: power up the rear motor, lift the bowl, close the apron, release the cushion hitch.
  • Never load the bowl unless the cushion hitch is locked down.

Self-loading

  • Make sure the cut area is clear of other scrapers.
  • Select and lock 1st or 2nd gear; cushion hitch down; bowl at ~router depth (100 mm).
  • Apply full throttle to both motors.
  • Check the cut depth allows continued momentum and hold full engine speed.
  • Don't "pump load" — cutting deeper loses momentum and the load falls back out.
